Staples in two letters

The foods you have logged now lead a search by how often you log them, not A–Z, so "ch" puts the chicken breast you log daily above the cheddar you logged once. Equal counts read A–Z, and everything you have never logged still follows A–Z.

Search leads with what you eat

A typed food search lists every match you have logged before first, A–Z, then every other match, A–Z. 0.9.0 v92–v101

The adaptability update

Squid stops assuming its user is a cyclist who lifts for hypertrophy, weighs in pounds and starts the week on Monday. A new account spends under a minute on setup and lands in an app shaped around what it tracks: any kind of cardio, four ways to measure training, its own units, and pages it can rearrange. Three stand-in users, a runner without power, a lifter who does no cardio and a cutter who logs only food and weight, each onboard, log a week and never meet anything built for a cyclist. Ian's app is unchanged apart from what was meant to change.

10 publishes tests 16 → 29 promises 155, each with a test slowest page at 4× 41 → 38 ms 2 data bugs found and fixed

Added

  • Onboarding. A new account opens on Quick setup, Full setup or Skip. Quick asks what you track, units and week start, bodyweight, goal direction, training style, and your kinds of cardio with their metrics and default, in about seven taps. Full is the Settings groups themselves, adds your MacroFactor history, and leaves out what changes nothing the app records. Answers save as you give them, so leaving part way keeps them. v98
  • Replay onboarding in Settings → Sample data. Runs every step on copies, writes nothing, and ends by listing what it would have set. v98
  • Week by week. A page and a dashboard card with one lane per kept domain: intake, weight, the training metric, cardio hours and cardio intensity (energy per hour), lined up on calendar weeks from your week start. A tap picks a week across every lane. 12 weeks, 26 or a year. v99
  • Arrange any page. Hold a section's heading on the weight, macros, expenditure, cardio, workouts or summary page to drag it or hide it with the eye. The Add menu works the same way. The layout syncs to the account. The bottom tabs never change. v97
  • Cardio of every kind. Ride, run, row, swim, walk and other, each with its own tracked metrics chosen from distance, heart rate, watts and MET, time always on. Energy is watts × 3.6 per hour where power is tracked and MET × bodyweight × hours otherwise; a kind with neither logs no energy. One kind is the default the logger opens on. v92, v96
  • The Cardio page with a filter by kind, and presets for every kind: a long run or a threshold ride goes on a day of the week and opens the logger filled in. v96, v97
  • Four training styles: effective reps, volume, strength over time (load × reps per exercise), or sessions only. The set sheet asks for what the style needs, and every strength view counts that style's metric. v92, v96
  • Hold to drag in the workout editor, the live session, the workouts and presets lists and the dashboard. Duplicate a workout. Exercises fold by body part under push, pull, legs and core, colour coded; rear delts pull, side delts push, low back is core. v93–v95
  • Units: distance in km or miles and energy in kcal or kJ, beside weight. A new account starts in its region's units and first day of the week. v92, v100
  • What you track. Food, bodyweight, strength and cardio each switch on and off; an untracked domain leaves the tabs, the dashboard and Settings. v92
  • Goal direction (lose, maintain, gain) in the renamed Goal and targets group. Stored for now; nothing reads it yet, by decision. v98
  • Kilojoule imports. A MacroFactor export headed "Energy (kJ)" is read and converted, and so is a generic CSV. The CSV export writes energy in your unit. v100

Changed

  • Empty states. Anything with nothing logged yet is its one action instead of zeros, dashes or a sentence about what it would show: a dashboard card becomes Log weight, Log a ride or Start a workout and a tap does it, once per action across the dashboard; a page's action moves from the bar into the body. v97
  • From 320 px to a tablet. Every grid column is strict, so content can't widen it. The dashboard goes to three columns once it has the room, and the last card on a short row takes what is left. Four-choice pickers go two by two when they don't fit; tab names shrink on a small phone at large text. v97, v98
  • Cardio speaks its own words. With one kind tracked the app uses that kind's words, so a rider still reads Riding; with more it says Cardio and sessions. Power things (FTP, TSS, presets' watts, the power dial) appear only where something tracks watts. An account with no cardio has no cardio column, count or row anywhere. v96, v100
  • Sessions-only style: a tap on a lift edits its sets, a swipe completes one. Effective reps are entered 0 to 5 by the swipe and are never copied from another set. v95, v97
  • Settings run in the onboarding order, with Appearance and Swipe and touch last, and lost the captions under weight, weekly basis, week start, sessions left open and the ride pool. v96
  • New accounts start neutral: no targets, no FTP, no power band, ride only until onboarding says otherwise. Existing accounts keep every number they hold. v92, v97
  • Sample data follows the account: sessions of its own kind, measured the way it measures them, and none at all where cardio isn't tracked. v100
  • Every small control is at least 30 px each way. v101

Removed

  • The derived effective-rep calculation. A set stores the effective reps entered, and only under that style. v92
  • The dashboard's arrange button and arrows, replaced by holding a card. v93–v95
  • Putting a kind of cardio itself on the week. It shipped in v96; a preset is what goes on a day instead. v97
  • "Nothing scheduled.", "No workouts yet.", "No photos yet.", "The plate is empty." and the other sentences explaining an absence. v97
  • Seven functions and one stylesheet rule nothing used. v101

Fixed

  • A slow connection could lose a re-entered record. Clearing a day and logging it again soon after could leave it missing from the phone, or showing the value from before, while the account had the right one. It predates v0.9. v100–v101
  • The onboarding replay left what the account delivered while it ran out of this device's cache. v101
  • The logging page scrolled sideways by 5 px at 320 px with large text. v97
  • The Add menu's squares left an empty slot when fewer than four were on. v97

Improved

  • The body-parts card walks the log once instead of once per part: same numbers, about four times faster. v101
  • The exercise page draws 150 nodes instead of 349 at three years, folded by body part. v95
  • The test suite: a configuration matrix over units, week start, theme, text size and density, data and width; three persona fixtures; a permanent release gate; an audit tool that measures two builds the same way. G1, G2, v100, v101

Hardening

Audit 3 found the app fast enough and the data path not: four ways to lose or keep data unintentionally, and a test suite that mostly could not fail. Nothing in 0.8.x adds a feature.

  • Sample data loaded from Settings was written to the account and came back as real after a reload. It now stays on the device. v82
  • Neither "everything" button erased everything, and they disagreed. One erase now clears every collection, photos and tombstones. v82

  • Renders coalesce to one per frame: a new device settles in two instead of nine. The demo waits for the account instead of flashing sample numbers at a returning user. v83
  • A change from another device rewrites one month of cache instead of all of it. One upload helper with progress and resume replaces six copies of the same loop. Comments are stripped from the published page. v83
  • The add-food page draws what is on screen first. The food log collapses empty hours outside your own logging window instead of opening on midnight. v83
  • The test suite, replaced: 16 tests, each proving lines in a behaviour spec, about 15 s for a full run, a text-parity tool for comparing builds, and the old 48 suites deleted. v83

0.8.0 ≈v58–v81 Reconstructed

The design system

Squid got a visual identity and a way for it to change without breaking. Colour enters one way: a component uses a role, a role points at a swatch, and a theme is a set of swatch values. Nothing below the palette holds a colour of its own, and a theme changes what a colour is, never what it means.

Added

  • The palette contract: 21 stored swatches (12 hues, 9 surfaces), about 50 roles reading them, every shadow and scrim from one swatch at six opacities. Text on a fill is derived to clear 4.5:1 from whatever the fill is, so an edited palette can't produce unreadable buttons.
  • Tokens for type scale, radius, border, shadow and motion, and text size and density as multipliers you set per device.
  • Themes: Default, High contrast, Nautical, Nocturne, Sepia, Crimson, Forest, Murica and Matrix, plus your own. Custom themes and the chosen one sync to the account, with a local copy for the first frame.
  • The theme editor with a live specimen and a colour picker built for the app. It never corrects a colour and never blocks saving; a real problem shows as a warning beside Save. Themes travel as pasted JSON.

Changed

  • The duplicated dark block merged; the categorical palettes tokenized and colliding roles separated, so the ten plan marks, the ten body parts and the four macros each stay distinct inside their own set.
  • Eight design principles, codified after v58 and v59 and applied since: the interface explains itself, position belongs to you, a colour only has to be distinct in its own set, grid before content, defaults from data, a look is not a decision, tapping a thing edits that thing, and consistency is a bug to fix.

Hero module leading the page, one Start a workout button in place of a pair that only looked like a pair, riding migrated onto the page as a section. Each chart keeps its own span and position; swiping sideways moves the window through time.

0.7.6

Per-segment bar tapping

The tap overlay converts the touch into chart space and returns the segment actually under it, instead of always answering with the topmost lift. The standing key was deleted; the readout became five data rows and no sentences.

0.7.5

Sample data status

Spinner and running count while a job writes, every button in the panel disabled until it finishes, and a confirmation on all seven paths. Six months is 182 day documents plus rides, each its own round trip. The wait was real work, not lag.

Bars split by the lift that produced them, so a body part's weekly number shows its own arithmetic. Weight on the bar separated into its own chart.

0.7.3

Sample data suite

Samples create the exercises they need rather than assuming a library. Three body profiles built forwards from a known expenditure using the same arithmetic the app tries to recover, so the estimator can be scored against the number that produced the weights.

Graph points only where a session actually trained the part, a hatched 20–30 band, and the volume metric dropped as redundant.

0.7.2

Sample data on demand

Launch seeding removed entirely. Six months of push, pull and legs, loaded from a button in settings, with tapering gains and a deload every seventh week so the lines have a shape to read rather than a ruler.

0.7.1

Sample seeding fix

Seeding at boot raced three ways: the demo could be up and its teardown cleared every collection, the upload ran before the database connected, and the account had not answered whether it already held training. A settings document written before the flag existed also reset it on every open, which would have deleted real sessions to lay samples over them.

0.7.0

Strength training

Effective reps after Lyle McDonald, workout plans, the live session with its set sheet and progress ring, the pattern-aware shuffle, and the post-workout report. Exercises carry a primary muscle at full credit and secondaries at a share.

The rep model was wrong twice before it was right. It ended flat, not peaked: a set of thirty and a set of eight look alike to the muscle, so the window fills by eight, holds to twelve, then gives way to three by thirty as breathing ends the set before the muscle does.

8 reps → 5.0 12 → 5.0 20 → 4.1 30 → 3.0
0.6.3

Multi-select editing

Tap an entry to enter selection mode; the menu replaces the search bar rather than stacking on it. Copy and Move arm the hour rows as drop targets. The week rail lost its stickiness entirely, which removed thirty lines of machinery along with the bug.

0.6.2

MacroFactor import

Both export formats parse end to end, food history feeds search, and recipe servings are corrected for MacroFactor writing serving weight as 100 ÷ servings. Pathways only, no data imported, by request.

0.6.1

Adaptive expenditure

A two-state Kalman filter over true weight and TDEE with an RTS smoother, drawn as a second line beside the window estimate. Weight moves on intake minus expenditure over 7700 kcal per kilogram, and the filter runs that backwards.

0.6.0

Expenditure rework

Window fixed at three weeks with the range moved into settings and the slider cut. Cardio modelled as a raised calorie ceiling, with each ride's calories forked and pooled across the days that follow rather than spiking one.

Graphs learned to tell a sideways swipe from an upright one, so panning a chart no longer fights scrolling the page.

Swipe-delete holds scroll position, stray calendar dots traced to a falsy gradient check, ride graphs turned red, and a pooled burned-per-day chart added. Riding became Workouts in the bottom nav with a hub behind it.

0.5.1

0.5.0

What each remaining step buys

Ahead

Migration

Out of the artifact. Everything to a GitHub repo, deployed on Cloudflare Pages and Workers, developed in Claude Code. It unblocks the AI proxy so no key ever sits in the page, a real nutrition API, theme files, the full-height add page, and a QA build with its own account for onboarding replays and destructive tests.

1.0.0

The polish update. One design language on every page, microanimations as theme variables, Themes 2.0 with gradients, radius, shadows and Google Fonts, first paint without flicker, and import and export finished.

After 1.0

Other people. Accounts, a merge strategy for sync, a privacy stance and a delete-my-data path before anyone but Ian signs in.
